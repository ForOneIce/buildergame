# Center paired buttons in equal halves

Date: 2026-09-12. AI tool: Codex.

## Original project instruction

一排有两个按钮时，一个在左半边的中间，一个在右半边的中间

## English translation (AI)

When a row contains two buttons, center one in the left half and the other in the right half.

## Preview interpretation (AI)

Paired action rows use two equal columns, with each content-sized button centered in its half. Narrow layouts may stack the actions when two buttons do not fit. This applies to paired action groups, not the three-tab navigation or compact account controls. Retain the concise popup, existing assets and brown-default/cream-selected button semantics.
