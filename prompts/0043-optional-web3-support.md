# Discuss optional Web3 support for builders and communities

Date: 2026-09-13 (UTC). AI tool: Codex. Status: discussion only; no integration approved.

## Original project instruction

下一个版本考虑如何和web3结合。能给builder提供支持或者给社区建设提供工具。我能想到一个扩展选项，是支持新建城镇时导入钱包地址，当用户探索小镇时hover到房屋旁的邮箱，鼠标指针会变成投币游戏图标。支持提供赞赏的方式。如果是个人小镇就只需要配置一个钱包地址。或者还有其他什么角度与当前赛道有关并且容易demo实现？先不要开发，和我讨论后再确定

## English translation (AI)

Consider how the next version could integrate Web3 to support builders or provide community-building tools. One optional extension would let creators supply wallet addresses when creating a town. When a visitor hovers over the mailbox beside a house, the pointer becomes a coin-insertion game icon, offering a way to tip. A personal town would need only one wallet address. What other approaches fit the current prize tracks and would be easy to demonstrate? Do not develop this yet; discuss it with me before deciding.

## Research output

Codex reread current official prize requirements and relevant ENS/Privy documentation, and assessed the existing static TypeScript frontend. [Options and sources](../docs/ideation/0043-web3-support-options.md) distinguish a generic wallet transfer from a qualifying partner integration. Mailbox tipping is the human's proposal; delegated ENS records, community grants and implementation boundaries are AI proposals. No SDK, wallet, contract or payment was created.
