# Give typography and layout room to breathe

## Original project instruction

字体排版间保留呼吸感，布局合理化，减低阅读理解困难

## English translation (AI)

Give the typography room to breathe, improve the layout, and reduce the effort needed to read and understand it.

## Preview interpretation (AI)

Increase the readability of body and secondary text, use generous line height and panel padding, and maintain a consistent spacing hierarchy. Keep labels close to their fields, group related information, and separate actions from surrounding copy. Reflow desktop and narrow layouts without clipping; use normal letter spacing for Chinese text. Exact dimensions and visual verification will be recorded after the proposal is revised and inspected.
