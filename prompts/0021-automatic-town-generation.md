# Automatic town generation research

Original instruction:

> 在设计城镇布局和道路时，你可以搜索有哪些现有的技术支持，或者继续使用建模工具搭建。这里有个疑问，之后按照不同主办人的输入，城市规模变大的形成需要怎么能够自动生成？这个有哪些方案

Translation: Research existing technology for town layouts and roads, or continue using modeling tools. How can towns automatically grow in scale according to different organizers' inputs, and what approaches are available?

Research and implementation recommendation: [automatic town growth options](../docs/procedural-town-plan.md). The assessment distinguishes generation for different initial collection sizes from later changes to a published collection, which the current snapshot contract disallows.
