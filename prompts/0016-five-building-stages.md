# 0016 — Expand the complete courtyard and derive five stages

## Original project instruction

!好可爱！就是这样的效果（如果可以的话，最后一个阶段的占地方块面积要越大，保持房屋主体不变，扩展外围圈地的范围，把外边的护栏做在离房子远一点的外围，这样屋子前的给客人看的立牌位置会更轻松一些，布局不那么局促。 立牌就可以做大一点，容纳得更多信息展示）。优化这一阶段，再生成其它阶段的。

## English translation

The current effect matches the intended direction. Enlarge the final stage's land tile while keeping the house itself unchanged. Move the perimeter fencing farther away so the visitor sign has more space; enlarge the sign to hold more information. Refine this stage, then generate the other stages.

## Scope

The existing visual direction is accepted. This authorizes a larger final courtyard and production of all five appearances. The revised art still requires review. See [implementation specification](../specs/0008-five-building-stages.md).
