# Developer towns as a second use case

Date: 2026-09-11. AI tool: Codex.

## Original project-related excerpt

The preceding administrative sentence is omitted; the following project proposal is preserved verbatim.

我又想到了一个华点。其实随着ai编程工具的加速迭代，个人开发者的github仓库数量也呈现指数级增长。这个buildergame不仅是黑客松主办方可以用。个人开发者也同样适合或者更适合使用。比如可以用来进行个人公开github仓库的公开展示和互动。这样项目使用范围又拓宽了，更符合buildergame的理念。

## English translation (AI)

I thought of another insight. As AI coding tools rapidly evolve, individual developers' GitHub repository counts are also growing exponentially. Buildergame is not only useful for hackathon organizers; individual developers may be equally or even better suited to it. They could use it to publicly showcase and enable interaction around their public GitHub repositories. This broadens the project's use cases and better fits the Buildergame concept.

## Attribution and evidence

- Human proposal: individual developers can exhibit their public repositories as towns.
- Exponential repository growth and stronger developer fit are hypotheses, not verified market findings.
- AI recommendation: reuse the town engine and curated manifest; retain the hackathon pilot and defer automated account imports.
